# Design Notes — YEONGSEO CHOI Portfolio

Reference: https://choiyeongseo-art.github.io/skp_portfolio_choiyeongseo/

## Visual direction
- Dark, editorial portfolio with a restrained, high-contrast look. The near-black background gives the artwork the focus and makes the typography feel gallery-like.
- The overall tone is confident and contemporary, mixing a clean portfolio presentation with a personal, artist-led identity.
- Images provide most of the expressive color. Some are monochrome portraits, while artwork and process imagery introduce saturated colors.

## Color
- **Base:** near-black / black backgrounds across the header and page sections.
- **Primary text:** white for section names and key headings; softer gray for Korean descriptions and secondary labels.
- **Accent:** bright cyan/turquoise for arrows, small section labels, selected heading words, and interactive emphasis.
- **Rules and borders:** subtle dark-gray dividers; occasional thin cyan outlines around process media.
- Keep interface colors sparse so the portfolio imagery remains the strongest source of color.

## Typography
- Large, bold, uppercase English display headings: `ABOUT`, `PROJECT`, `DRAWING`. Their geometric, compact sans-serif feel gives the page a strong editorial hierarchy.
- Small uppercase English eyebrow labels use wider tracking and a bold weight (for example, section numbering and category descriptors).
- Korean supporting copy is smaller, lighter in weight, and gray, with comfortable line spacing.
- Navigation and calls to action are compact uppercase text. Cyan arrow marks add emphasis without making the controls visually heavy.
- The screenshot suggests a modern sans-serif family; exact font family was not identifiable from visual inspection.

## Spacing and layout
- A slim top header places the `YC®` mark at the left and a compact outlined `MENU +` control at the right, separated from the page by a fine horizontal rule.
- A second narrow band carries the portfolio name on the left and disciplines on the right. Small uppercase text is aligned to the outer page margins.
- The opening area uses generous dark negative space, creating a deliberate pause before the portfolio content.
- The three main entry sections use alternating two-column compositions: image and text switch sides from one section to the next. Wide gutters and substantial vertical gaps keep each section distinct.
- Text blocks are relatively narrow. Eyebrow label, large heading, short description, and underlined `EXPLORE` link form a consistent vertical stack.
- A horizontal divider introduces the process-preview area. Its small meta labels sit at opposite ends, with the Korean section title centered beneath and the media preview below it.
- The page uses thin lines, restrained borders, and generous section spacing rather than boxed cards or dense panels.

## Interaction details observed
- `MENU +` is a small outlined button in the header.
- `EXPLORE` links use a fine underline and cyan arrow accent.
- The process preview has previous/next controls and a three-item selector.
- A floating chatbot prompt appears near the lower-right edge; it uses a cyan outline and a bright cyan label.

## Responsive impression
- In the narrow browser view, the layout retains its black canvas, clear typography, image-led sections, and generous vertical rhythm. Content appears stacked or adapted for a narrow viewport; desktop alignment should preserve the alternating image/text rhythm where space allows.
